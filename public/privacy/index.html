Lingo Guess

Privacy

You can play every part of Lingo Guess without an account, and without cookies. The only reason an account exists is the Daily Archive — the calendar of days you have stamped.

Playing signed out: no cookies, no third-party scripts, no account. Your archive and best score live in this browser's local storage, on this device.

If you sign in with Google

  • You are sent to Google to approve the sign-in. No Google script runs on this site.
  • We receive and store your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture URL, so we can recognise you next time and greet you by name.
  • We store your archive and game history — one JSON record of the days you stamped, the score of each day, and the languages you heard.
  • We set one first-party session cookie so you stay signed in. It is HttpOnly and lasts 90 days.
  • We never receive your Google password, and we do not keep Google access or refresh tokens.

Where it is stored

On our own server, hosted by Hostinger in the European Union. Analytics are first-party and aggregate: no raw IP addresses are stored, and events are deleted after 400 days.

Deleting it

Open your archive and choose Delete my data. That removes your account and every day stored with it from our server immediately — it is a hard delete, not a flag. Anything still in this browser's local storage stays on your device until you clear it.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no tracking pixels, no data sold or shared with third parties.
  • No third-party analytics, fonts or scripts.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured.
